# Brocoberries – AI-Assisted Children’s Learning Workbook

Building AI course project

## Summary

Brocoberries uses AI to help create English learning stories, illustrations, and activities for German-speaking children aged 5–8. Human review ensures educational quality and age-appropriate content.

Background

Creating an engaging educational workbook requires time, creativity, and careful planning.

The main challenges are:

* Developing age-appropriate English learning activities.
* Creating engaging stories and illustrations.
* Maintaining consistent characters throughout the book.
* Checking the accuracy and quality of educational content.

My motivation was to create an enjoyable learning experience for children while exploring how AI could support educational content development.

How is it used?

The workbook features two characters, Robin and Hugo, who guide children through English alphabet activities.

The development process includes:

1. Planning the educational goals and workbook structure.
2. Using generative AI tools to assist with story and illustration development.
3. Creating handwriting, vocabulary, and other learning activities.
4. Reviewing and correcting the content and visual consistency.
5. Preparing the workbook for publication.

Parents and children are the intended users of the finished workbook.

Data sources and AI methods

This project explores the practical use of generative AI, including:

* Large language models for drafting and improving educational text.
* AI image generation for illustrations and visual concepts.
* Human evaluation to check educational accuracy, readability, and consistency.

The project uses original educational concepts, prompts, and human-directed design decisions.

It does not involve training a new neural network or collecting personal data from children.

Challenges

AI-generated content can contain errors or inconsistent illustrations.

AI does not automatically guarantee that an activity is educationally appropriate for every child.

All content requires human review. Copyright, privacy, and responsible AI use must also be considered.

What next?

Possible future developments include:

* Expanding the workbook into a series of educational books.
* Testing activities with parents and educators.
* Exploring AI-assisted recommendations for different learning levels.
* Improving accessibility and multilingual learning support.

These are future ideas rather than features already implemented.
